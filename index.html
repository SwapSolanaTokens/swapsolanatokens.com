🪐 About

One page, two ways to move your assets

Swap Solana Tokens is an integrated platform combining two things you'd normally need two separate sites for: swapping SPL tokens on Solana, and bridging assets in from other chains. Neither is a standalone exchange or bridge of our own — both panels are focused front-ends running on official third-party plugins and APIs.

The Swap panel runs on Jupiter's plugin and API, the same aggregation engine routing the majority of swap volume across the Solana ecosystem. No order book of our own, no extra smart-contract risk — every quote and every fill comes straight from Jupiter's liquidity.

The Bridge panel, right next to it, runs on deBridge, an independent, non-custodial cross-chain protocol — not affiliated with Jupiter or with Swap Solana Tokens. It lets you move assets from Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum and other chains directly into Solana (or back out again), so you can bridge in and swap on the same page.

💬 FAQ

Common questions

Is this an official Jupiter or deBridge product?

No, to both. This is an independent front-end: the swap panel plugs into Jupiter's official plugin and public price API, and the bridge panel plugs into deBridge's official widget. We're not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by either Jupiter Exchange or deBridge. All routing, execution and pricing come straight from each protocol's own infrastructure.

Does this site custody my funds?

Never. Swaps settle wallet-to-wallet through Jupiter's audited smart contracts. We never touch your funds or private keys at any point.

Why is there a referral fee?

A 0.5% fee, Jupiter's minimum, is baked into the quote to help run this front-end. What you see is what you pay — no surprises at signing.

Can I bring tokens from other chains?

Yes. Next to the Jupiter swap panel there's a separate bridge widget powered by deBridge, a non-custodial cross-chain protocol independent from Jupiter. It lets you move assets from Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum and other chains into Solana, or the other way around. A 0.3% fee is baked into the quote there too — this is deBridge's own fee mechanism, separate from Jupiter's referral fee above.

Why do I see extra wallet buttons on mobile?

On a regular mobile browser (Chrome/Safari), the Swap panel sometimes can't detect your wallet. Tap the Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack button to open the swap inside that wallet's own browser and connect normally. The Bridge panel doesn't have this limitation and works the same on any browser.

Which wallets can I use?

Depends on the panel. For the Swap panel, any Solana wallet Jupiter's plugin supports — Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and others — connect right inside that widget. For the Bridge panel, deBridge supports wallets on the chain you're bridging from, including MetaMask and other EVM wallets on Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum and more, plus a Solana wallet to receive on this end. You'll pick the right wallet the moment you hit connect in each panel.

What if a swap fails or slips?

Network congestion or fast-moving prices can occasionally push a trade past your slippage tolerance, and it'll fail safely. Nothing is deducted — just try again.

How does the price chart work?

The chart is an embedded, public Birdeye widget — the same charting engine used across the Solana ecosystem — showing candlestick pricing for the token currently loaded in the Jupiter swap panel. It doesn't track the deBridge bridge panel. It starts on SOL, but the moment you pick a different output token (either inside the swap widget or by tapping a coin in the scrolling ticker above), the chart swaps to that token's most liquid market automatically, no page reload needed.

Use the 15m / 1H / 4H / 1D buttons to change the candle interval — this only changes how the chart is drawn, it never affects your trade. Pricing and candle data come straight from Birdeye's public infrastructure, not from us, so the chart is for informational purposes only and can lag by a few seconds behind the live quote you see in the swap widget. Always double check the quoted price and route before confirming a swap.
